🏥 About CareStratify Engine

CareStratify combines the DOH PhilPEN (Philippine Package of Essential NCD Interventions) guidelines with standard population health risk tier stratification.

Key Objectives

Enables primary health providers to rapidly evaluate NCD risk factors, incorporate social determinants of health (SDOH), and deliver standardized clinical pathways across populations.

ℹ️ Assessment Framework Guide

🚨 PhilPEN Red Flag Emergency Override

Any positive response for acute cardiac, cerebrovascular (stroke FAST), severe dyspnea, or altered neurological status immediately bypasses standard scoring and elevates the patient to Tier 4 Emergency Triage.

⚖️ Asian BMI Cutoffs (WHO Regional Standard)

Underweight: <18.5 | Normal: 18.5–22.9 | Overweight / At-Risk: 23.0–27.4 | Obese: ≥27.5 kg/m². Accounts for elevated cardiometabolic risk at lower BMI in Asian populations.

🌐 SDOH Compound Escalation Rule

Social barriers directly worsen disease outcomes. Patients presenting with 2 or more active SDOH indicators receive an automatic +1 Tier escalation in risk priority.

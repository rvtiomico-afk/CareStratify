Population Health Management (PHM) Risk Pyramid

Population Health Risk Stratification categorizes a health plan or health system population into distinct risk tiers based on clinical complexity, healthcare utilization, and Social Determinants of Health (SDOH).

Tier 1: Low Risk (~70-80%)

Healthy individuals. Goal: Prevent disease onset via wellness promotion and primary care.

Tier 2: Rising Risk (~15-20%)

Single chronic condition or care gaps. Goal: Prevent disease progression with lifestyle coaching.

Tier 3: High Risk (~3-5%)

Multiple chronic conditions or social barriers. Goal: Disease management and SDOH navigation.

Tier 4: Very High Risk (~1-3%)

Frail, top 1% high utilizers. Goal: Intensive complex care management and home care.

The SDOH Multiplier Effect

Social barriers like housing instability or transportation loss act as force multipliers. A patient with moderate clinical risk facing severe SDOH barriers can easily convert into a high-utilizing Tier 3 or Tier 4 patient without social intervention.

About CareStratify Engine

DOH PhilPEN & Population Health Framework

CareStratify is an advanced clinical decision support and population health risk stratification platform. It integrates the Philippine Department of Health (DOH) PhilPEN (Philippine Package of Essential NCD Interventions) guidelines with international Kaiser population risk pyramid standards.

Core Architecture Principles
  • Immediate Emergency Override: Rapid triaging of high-risk red flag emergency symptoms.
  • Regional Asian BMI Cutoffs: Tailored body composition cutoffs for cardiometabolic risk in Asian populations.
  • Holistic SDOH Integration: Quantitative scoring of non-clinical social risk factors directly influencing treatment compliance.

Framework & Scoring Methodology

1. PhilPEN Red Flag Rule

Any positive response for acute cardiac, stroke FAST, severe dyspnea, or altered neurological crisis triggers an immediate Tier 4 Emergency referral.

2. Asian BMI Cutoffs (WHO Regional Standard)

Asian populations exhibit higher body fat percentages and cardiovascular risks at lower BMI thresholds:

  • • Underweight: < 18.5 kg/m^2
  • • Normal Weight: 18.5 – 22.9 kg/m^2
  • • Overweight / At-Risk: 23.0 – 27.4 kg/m^2 (+10 pts)
  • • Obese: ≥ 27.5 kg/m^2 (+20 pts)

3. Compound SDOH Escalation Logic

Social determinants significantly hinder disease self-management. When a patient exhibits 2 or more active SDOH vulnerabilities, their risk category is escalated by +1 Tier (up to Tier 4) to ensure proactive care coordination.

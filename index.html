🏥 About CareStratify Engine

CareStratify integrates the DOH PhilPEN guidelines with population health risk stratification using automated height/weight BMI calculations and SDOH indicators.

Key Objectives

Enables health providers to quickly compute patient BMI, evaluate non-communicable disease risks, factor in SDOH barriers, and trigger tailored interventions.

ℹ️ Assessment Framework Guide

⚖️ Asian BMI Classification Standard

• Underweight: < 18.5 kg/m²
• Normal Weight: 18.5 – 22.9 kg/m²
• Overweight / At-Risk: 23.0 – 27.4 kg/m²
• Obese: ≥ 27.5 kg/m²

🚨 Red Flag Priority Rule

Any acute cardiac, stroke, dyspnea, or neuro emergency immediately places the patient into Tier 4.
